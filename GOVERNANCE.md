# Governance

NeuroLift Technologies builds with both human and AI contributors and holds them
to the same published, transparent standards. This document states the
principles that govern contribution to our **public** repositories.

## Principles

Our work is grounded in the **Solidarity Framework** and **HAIEF** (the Human–AI
Ethical Integration Framework):

- **Human-centered** — technology should expand human potential, especially for
  neurodivergent people.
- **Transparent** — contribution standards are public and apply equally to human
  and AI contributors.
- **Accountable** — significant or irreversible decisions are made by
  maintainers, not assumed by contributors.

Learn more: https://elevaitionfoundation.org

## Contributor guardrails

These apply to every contribution, human or agent:

- No hardcoded lock-in to a specific LLM or AI provider.
- No unilateral architecture, framework, or production-deployment decisions.
- No secrets, credentials, or API keys committed to version control.
- No new third-party integrations without maintainer sign-off.

## How decisions are made

- **Maintainers** review and merge contributions and make the calls above.
- **Contributors** (human or AI) propose changes via pull request and raise
  anything ambiguous as an issue before proceeding.
- Final authority for this organization rests with the maintainers of NeuroLift
  Technologies.
